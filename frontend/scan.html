    Migration horizon

    The score says how much vulnerable cryptography you have. It cannot say whether you finish migrating in time. Mosca's inequality can: X + Y > Z means data sent today is already exposed, because an adversary records it now and decrypts it once Z arrives.

    What this refuses to claim

    Z is unknowable, so nothing here predicts it. The slider is your scenario, not a forecast. The figure that needs no guess is the break-even year: the latest a quantum computer could arrive while still leaving time to migrate. That is a property of your code and your data, not of the future.

    Y is an estimate, so it is reported as a range. Effort is counted per cryptographic family rather than per finding, because the expensive part is the design decision and that is paid once; call sites then scale sub-linearly.
